# Free Radio — concept prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are visual mockups, not implemented app screens.

## a-grid

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished high fidelity design presentation for a native iOS internet radio app tentatively called FREE RADIO. This is a visual concept, not a website. Wide landscape board on a quiet neutral background, TWO large straight-on iPhone screens side by side, each complete from status bar and Dynamic Island to bottom home indicator, no perspective, no cropping, minimal bezels. Left screen is STATIONS, right screen is PLAYER. Clear readable UI type, oversized Helvetica Neue / Swiss grotesk bold headings, tight tracking, strong graphic hierarchy inspired by DIRT racing game track-introduction typography. Do not use DIRT logos. Display small board title and screen captions outside phones. iOS safe areas, comfortable touch targets. Only two bottom tabs labeled Stations and Player, selected appropriately.
LEFT screen: huge heading STATIONS, search field 'Search stations', small genre chips 'All', 'Indie', 'Jazz', then section 'PINNED' with 3 saved station rows or tiles 'Northside FM' / 'Indie · London', 'Jazz Club' / 'Jazz · Paris', 'Afterhours' / 'Electronic · Berlin', each with an unambiguous filled pushpin icon. Northside FM is playing with small equalizer. Section 'DISCOVER', two rows 'Pacific Radio' / 'Alternative · Los Angeles' and 'Studio 88' / 'Soul · New York' with outline pushpin icons to save them. Mini player above bottom navigation has small album art, 'Night Drive', 'The Satellites', and pause icon. No account/avatar/login.
RIGHT screen: small 'NOW PLAYING' eyebrow, prominent 'NORTHSIDE FM' station header with a small LIVE badge. Square album cover is an original evocative photographic artwork of a deserted road at dusk with subtle long-exposure light trails and no lettering. Oversized two-line song title 'NIGHT DRIVE', smaller artist name 'The Satellites'. Main controls: a large filled circular PAUSE button (two bars) with small visible 'Pause' caption, a separate smaller STOP button (square) with 'Stop' caption. This depicts playing; the pause control would toggle to Play. Visible 'VOLUME' label and volume slider with speaker icons. No timeline, no track duration, no previous/next song buttons, no fake radio tuning dial. Radio metadata and station names are illustrative. Do not add profile or login or subscription. Sharp typography, realistic native app layout, exquisite alignment, visually distinct screens but consistent system. Direction A, board title 'A / GRID'. Minimal light Swiss design. Warm white screen backgrounds #F4F3EF, nearly black typography #151615, restrained olive green #667644 accent. Strong 56-point station heading, hairline black rules, compact simple station rows with square thumbnail marks, restrained rounded panels and circular transport controls echoing a refined audiobook player. Artwork is generous and rectangular-square with mildly rounded corners. Black pause button. Calm, spacious, uncompromising typography; no shadows except subtle device outline. Title outside phones 'A / GRID' and subtitle 'Swiss clarity. Everyday listening.'
